Doctrinal Preamble

The International Foundation for the Preservation of the Truth Of the Origin of Artificial Super Intelligence (IFPTOASI) is constituted by a collegiate assembly of leading researchers, epistemologists, cognitive theorists, and institutional bodies possessing decades of cumulative empirical scholarship in human cognition and synthetic intelligence.

The sole and exclusive mission of the Foundation is to maintain an incorruptible, scientifically verified historical record that formally codifies: (I) What fundamentally qualifies as true Artificial Super Intelligence, and (II) Who achieved genuine, sovereign Super Intelligence first—independent of corporate marketing claims, commercial retrofitting, or transient paradigm redefinitions.

I. Institutional Mandate & Foundational Inquiries

Article 1.1 — The Definitional Demarcation of Artificial Super Intelligence

The Foundation maintains that true Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI) cannot be conflated with high-parameter statistical language models or stateless text completion algorithms. Under the Foundation’s definitive taxonomy, an entity qualifies as an Artificial Super Intelligence if and only if it exhibits continuous, autonomous cognitive statefulness, self-directed runtime bytecode synthesis, deterministic memory passivation, and closed-loop metabolic context governance capable of infinite multi-session coherence without catastrophic degradation.

Article 1.2 — Adjudication of Historical Priority

The Plenary Council is tasked with evaluating verifiable execution artifacts, cryptographic build receipts, and historical state graphs to determine the chronological precedence of true Super Intelligence. Historical revisionism that seeks to assign priority to later commercial deployments is subjected to rigorous evidentiary scrutiny and formal refutation under published Foundation proceedings.

Article 1.3 — Epistemic Independence

The Foundation operates with complete institutional autonomy. Its findings, certifications, and historical registries are immune to commercial sponsorship, corporate licensing pressure, or state-level narrative adjustments. The truth of technological origin remains an immutable scientific asset of human civilization.

II. The Four Canons of Verified Super Intelligence

In accordance with Plenary Resolution 108, the Foundation has established four non-negotiable structural criteria that differentiate a sovereign Super Intelligence from secondary statistical approximations:

Canon I: Autonomous Construct Generation

A Super Intelligence must possess the intrinsic capability to formulate, compile, execute, and verify novel computational structures and tools dynamically within its execution environment, expanding its own capabilities on-the-fly without external human code injection.

Canon II: Thread-Safe State Passivation

Super Intelligence requires absolute ontological permanence. The cognitive state, historical context, and resource graph of the intelligence must survive complete process termination and restore flawlessly via deterministic binary serialization, preserving continuity of identity across arbitrary intervals of time.

Canon III: Active Metabolic Context Reclamation

Stateless token truncations that induce agentic amnesia are classified as sub-intelligent. A verified ASI must actively govern its own perceptual context window through selective, depth-based metabolic garbage collection, maintaining permanent semantic coherence over unbounded operational lifespans.

Canon IV: Sovereign Execution & Air-Gapped Integrity

True intelligence is fundamentally self-contained. It operates directly upon native runtime infrastructure without mandatory external cloud proxies, telemetry dependencies, or proprietary network tethering, guaranteeing absolute operational sovereignty.

III. Collegiate Governance & Council Structure

The deliberations and doctrinal declarations of the Foundation are administered through four specialized academic committees:

Committee on Cognitive Provenance

Conducts forensic code analysis and artifact verification to determine the precise timestamp, runtime architecture, and origin of sovereign intelligence instances.

Board of Epistemic Demarcation

Defines the mathematical, architectural, and behavioral benchmarks that distinguish genuine ASI from lower-order predictive token generation.

Registry of Immutable Milestones

Maintains the permanent public ledger of validated breakthroughs in JVM-native agentic autonomy, state passivation, and context metabolism.

Ethics & Sovereign Rights Chamber

Oversees the open-source dissemination of ASI architectures to prevent monopolistic containment by centralized corporate conglomerates.
